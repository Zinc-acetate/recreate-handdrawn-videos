# Public Methods and Boundaries

Use these sources to trace methods and judge their applicability alongside this skill's timing, motion, and review guidance. The independently worded summaries do not bundle third-party source code or install another skill.

- [CXP-2024: Source analysis and exposure mapping](https://github.com/CXP-2024/pv-character-replacement-skill/blob/main/skills/pv-character-replacement/references/analysis-and-timing.md): distinct-drawing identification, two-dimensional difference aids, and source-to-replacement exposure mapping. Useful for low-frame-rate cel recreation; automated candidates still require review.
- [CXP-2024: Layers and continuity](https://github.com/CXP-2024/pv-character-replacement-skill/blob/main/skills/pv-character-replacement/references/layers-and-continuity.md): fixed canvases, independent layer timing, hair continuity, and strict versus adapted exposure. Adaptation is a documented change, not proof of exact fidelity.
- [CXP-2024: Identity and keyframes](https://github.com/CXP-2024/codex-pv-character-replacement-skill/blob/main/skills/codex-pv-character-replacement/references/keyframes.md): separate roles for source frames, identity references, and accepted drawings; shot-specific linework and shading budgets.

These repositories emphasize different workflows; do not treat them as mirrors of one tool. Mentioned models, workflow backends, and helper scripts are not necessarily bundled here or installed in the current environment. Verify capabilities and authorization before use. Analysis helpers do not generate replacement drawings or replace original-speed review. Do not generalize a model's anecdotal success or a fixed palette size to every hand-drawn video.


- [CXP-2024 spatial-difference prompting](https://github.com/CXP-2024/pv-character-replacement-skill/blob/main/skills/pv-character-replacement/references/prompt-patterns.md) and [proportion/placement diagnosis](https://github.com/CXP-2024/pv-character-replacement-skill/blob/main/skills/pv-character-replacement/references/proportions-and-placement.md): reference-role boards, regional change descriptions, and placement-versus-structure repair decisions. Independently synthesized here as visual-workflow checks, not copied templates or scripts; garment folds still follow replacement design and source pose.
